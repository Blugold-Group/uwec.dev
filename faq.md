### What computer do I need?

Just about any laptop running Windows 11 (or a distribution Linux if you're feeling adventurous 😏) should do. Most classes take advantage of campus servers for most computation related tasks (such as compiling code, hosting databases, running LLMs) which can handle _far_ more than what is needed for classes.

### Why join clubs?

Instant friends cheat. You surround yourself with people that have similar interests which leads to friends! Also, clubs offer networking opportunities, some clubs bring in guest speakers who you can connect with and gain connections in the industry.

### How should I use AI in class?

AI is a constantly evolving topic and we recognize that as developers use it more and more to write code. However, you still need to understand what it writes. The best way to do that is by writing code yourself and using AI as a tool to learn how to write code rather than letting it write code for you.

### How am I going to get a job if AI exists?

While AI has a major impact on the job market increasing unemployment due to AI related layoffs. Where there are computers to be used, there will be jobs. When the calculator was invented mathematicians were scared that they were going to take over and everyone was going to lose their jobs. Now it's a common tool.

### How do I choose what field to go into?

Trying a variety of things is the best way to find what interests you. Try a wide variety of classes, go to clubs, search the internet.
